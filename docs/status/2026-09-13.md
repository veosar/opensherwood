- Governance: ADR-0009, the two-tier wall in AGENTS.md / legal.md, the `analyst-ghidra` skill, the spec
  template, the gate against decompiler output in `check_no_assets.py`, the Ghidra export scripts.

  full decompilation exported for the analysts.
- **Implementer batch 1 (ruleset 19, 2026-09-18)**: the single logic frame of ADR-0010 and the script VM's
  interpreter core, from the cleared parts of `spec-script-vm.md` revision 9.
  - One clock: `TICK_RATE` = (64, 3) Hz, 46.875 ms. The 64 Hz animation sub-clock and the 25 Hz script
    sub-clock are gone with their conversions; animation timers, AI timers, script timers, the auto-save
    period, the replay checkpoints, the harness's `tick_rate` and every constant that was expressed in ticks
    now count frames. The `TickRate` and `FrameLength` assumptions disappeared with them.
  - The interpreter is rebuilt against section 3.1: one instruction per bytecode quad, the operand composition
    of VM-002, `0x07` returning at once, the persistent result slot, the native result register, the
    script-parameter and 12-cell native argument buffers with their cross-callback persistence, the float
    opcodes over the cells' bits with the unordered comparison outcomes, and the four storage classes.
  - The native call table follows section 6 for all 265 ids: arity, bool-converted arguments and result
    convention per id; settled rows implemented; rows whose effect reaches a subsystem this engine models only
    in part keep its behaviour behind the new protocol and record `Policy(id)`; the ids of 4.2 answer their
    prescribed placeholder (`UnknownNative(id)`) and those of 4.3 pass their value through
    (`UnresolvedEffect(id)`).
  - Messages are synchronous (3.6), with the dynamic current actor and current scroll of VM-093 / VM-094.
  - The failure classes of VM-089 get the outcomes of 8.1, with a hashed fault log, the three suppression sets
    and the sequence provenance of 8.3; a terminated callback keeps what it did.
  - Not cleared, so unchanged behind the new interfaces: the scheduler (3.5), the sequence machinery (3.7) and
    every native whose effect depends on the camera, animation, AI, navigation or campaign specifications.
