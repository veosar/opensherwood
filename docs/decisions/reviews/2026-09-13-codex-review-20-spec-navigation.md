1. **Blocker — NAV-121, NAV-141, NAV-144 — expression filter still fails.** Addresses: `0x004f9d60`, `0x00556490`, `0x00554b80`, `0x005553d0`. Offending passages include “its predecessor becomes this edge, `g_A = g + cost`”, “`A` is (re)opened”, and “rotate … clockwise … and counter-clockwise in lockstep, each rotation stopping”. These reproduce recovered bookkeeping and branch-by-branch control flow. **Correction:** express cost comparisons, candidate selection, tie outcomes and final waypoint sequences as behavioral requirements; remove the prescribed mutations and lockstep procedure. The automated asset check passing does not clear these passages.

2. **Blocker — NAV-046/057, NAV-141, NAV-171/200; §§4, 9 — essential implementation requirements remain unread.** Addresses: `0x00558210`, `0x005557c0`, `0x0054eea0`, `0x004d28e0`, `0x004d7880`, `0x0046a900`, `0x00469770`, `0x0046a000`. Goal validity, patch targeting and eviction, player door interaction, and precise crossing transitions remain missing. Referring to the movement specification also leaves sliding and proximity responses open. **Correction:** complete these requirements, or explicitly narrow the implementation-clearance scope and provide identified assumptions with defined fallback behavior. “Must be an `Assumption` variant until read” does not supply an executable contract.

3. **Blocker — NAV-055/056, NAV-141/147; §8.2 — masks alone do not preserve search ordering.** Addresses: `0x00555a10`, `0x00553e60`, `0x00556490`, `0x0055aa60`. Availability transitions remove and reinsert nodes and edges, changing traversal order. Identical state words can therefore accompany different candidate ordering, which matters under the specified tie rules. **Correction:** specify the observable ordering after toggles and restore. Preserve the necessary ordering state, or declare and approve a canonical-order deviation. Add a toggle–snapshot–restore test with equal-cost alternatives.

4. **High — NAV-057 — completed-but-unconsumed results are misdescribed.** Addresses: `0x004d28e0`, `0x00554750`, `0x005546e0`. “Search results computed before the toggle are not revised” is false for a completed request still awaiting consumption: restarting the worker selects the queued head again, so it can be searched against the changed geometry. **Correction:** distinguish queued, executing, completed-but-unconsumed, and already-consumed requests, and specify patch behavior for each.

5. **High — NAV-090/147; §8.1 — the scheduling deviation is presented as established timing equivalence.** Addresses: `0x005532a0`, `0x004d23d0`, `0x005546e0`. “Its only visible effects are NAV-090 and NAV-147” and “a request never waits longer than in the original” are unsupported. Thread completion timing changes delivery opportunities; patch restart is another dispatch opportunity. ADR-0004 requires determinism but does not approve this particular replacement. **Correction:** label the schedule as a proposed deterministic policy, define all dispatch and delivery phases, and resolve cancellation, patch restart and work-budget behavior. Under the ordinary phases stated here, a request created during tick `t` starts at `t+1` and delivers at `t+2` at the earliest.

6. **High — §8.2, NAV-140/146/147 — snapshot coverage remains incomplete.** Addresses: `0x0055aa60`, `0x004d23d0`; ADR-0004 snapshot invariant. The contract does not explicitly cover completed pending results and their delivery tick, full queued-request inputs and owner associations, or the geometry state against which a result was computed. Re-running from inputs against subsequently changed geometry need not reproduce the pending result. **Correction:** define each authoritative request/result state, its canonical hash coverage, and restore behavior; test snapshots before dispatch, before delivery, and across a patch.

7. **High — NAV-110; §7.2 test 2 — clearance omits the map-bounds condition.** Addresses: `0x004f5770`, `0x00608f10`, `0x006082a0`. The initial check tests overlap with the level rectangle, not merely whether the supplied box is nonempty. A valid box wholly outside the map fails. **Correction:** distinguish “outside all sectors but overlapping the map” from “outside the map”, and specify partial-overlap and boundary cases.

8. **High — NAV-111 — unsticking still uses the wrong displacement rule.** Addresses: `0x004f5890`, `0x0051fdc0`. “Along the axis of least penetration” and “the four axis directions are tried” are unsupported. The displacement follows an oriented wall normal, with box-corner projections; diagonal walls can produce diagonal corrections. **Correction:** describe the resulting separation requirement, sidedness, tolerances and ordering effects, with diagonal-wall and multiple-wall examples. Retaining the correct 50-round limit does not resolve this error.

9. **High — NAV-143; §7.2 test 4 — quadrant boundary inequalities are reversed.** Addresses: `0x00553a10`, particularly `0x00553b42`–`0x00553c52`. For candidate bit 1 at `(94,96)`, `Q=(94,120)` fails, while `Q=(50,96)` passes—the opposite of the new expectations. **Correction:** rederive the axis-boundary ownership from both accepted orientations and replace the formula and tests. Include both axes for all four candidate bits.

10. **High — NAV-144/145; §7.2 test 5 — reconstruction direction is ambiguous and the claimed waypoint order is wrong under the stated helper inputs.** Addresses: `0x005547c0`, `0x00554b80`, `0x005553d0`, `0x00610a00`. For the backward helper case described as `R=1, L=4`, the final travel order is `4,8,1`, not `1,8,4`. **Correction:** define candidate sets by the start-facing and goal-facing connections and give final forward sequences. Separately specify the initial graph-node case; its corner walk is not centered on the literal start point.

11. **High — NAV-121; §7.2 test 8 — the link filter is still overgeneralized.** Address: `0x004f9d60`. The comparison uses the two doors’ corresponding serialized sides selected by the current crossing direction. It does not generally compare their geometrically opposite sectors after independently orienting both doors. Reversing one door’s A/B orientation can therefore change the result. **Correction:** settle this orientation-dependent requirement and supply same-orientation and reversed-orientation examples. The current “both lead from Y to X” rule does not establish it.

12. **High — NAV-150; §7.2 test 12 — approach failure and the sector-target branch are missing.** Address: `0x00467a50`, type-2 case. 13. **High — NAV-150(b) — proximity geometry remains incorrect.** Addresses: `0x005613a6`–`0x0056140b`, `0x00608400`. 14. **Medium — NAV-300; §6 natives 182, 186–189; §8.4 — invalid-handle behavior remains unspecified.** Addresses: `0x00579810`, `0x00579850`, `0x00579870`, `0x00579880`, `0x005798a0`. 15. **Medium — NAV-300, native 64; §8.4 — nearest-door selection lacks necessary precision, and an original filter is mislabeled a deviation.** Addresses: `0x0057492a`–`0x00574956`, `0x005fc6d0`.
