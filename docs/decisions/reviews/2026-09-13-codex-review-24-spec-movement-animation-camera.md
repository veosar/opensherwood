11. **High — proximity and arrival geometry — ANIM-240, 242; §12 NAV amendments — addresses: 00561040, 00560460, 005fc660, 00467a50, 005fc700.** The asserted center-distance proximity test and plain radial arrival test are wrong. Both relevant comparisons use a **directional dot product**: proximity tests the partner displacement’s forward projection against 5; plain arrival compares the remaining displacement’s projection with the stopping tolerance. The separate approach test uses **Chebyshev distance, strictly less than tolerance + 5**. Preserve the additional eligibility guards and keep unread geometric cases excluded.

12. **Blocker — camera location conversion — ANIM-320, 330–332; §6; tests 16–17 — addresses: 005758d0, 00571270, 00571330, 005713f0, 004ca410.** The shared helper does more than clamp the supplied point. It first converts the location to a corner by subtracting half the **configured screen dimensions divided by zoom**, then truncates and applies boundary handling; zoom 0.5 also enforces even coordinates. Its small-map fallback can set current zoom to 1.0. Consequently, “corner equals the clamped point” and unconditional “zoom unchanged” are incomplete. Specify the conversion and exceptional case, and choose acceptance destinations accordingly.
